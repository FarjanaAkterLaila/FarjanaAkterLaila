### 👤 Who I am

I'm a **Mid-level Developer** working on **web & mobile apps**, who likes taking messy problems and shaping them into clear, lovely, easy-to-use products.

- 📷 Software Developer at **[XIIA](https://www.linkedin.com/company/xiia)** - that's where the work happens day to day
- ⚛ Building for the browser and the phone with **React, Next.js, and React Native**
- 🖌 Caring about how it looks and feels - tight UI, smooth motion, thoughtful UX
- 🗄 Also covering the backend: **Node.js, Express, Firebase, and MongoDB**
- 📚 Always learning, always sharpening the craft
- ✅ Finishing work and putting it in people's hands

### 💼 How I spend my days

- 🎯 Take Figma files from screen to live web and mobile apps
- 🎛 Style interfaces with Tailwind, Styled Components, and Framer Motion
- 🔗 Wire products to REST APIs, GraphQL, and Firebase
- 📲 Put React Native apps in people's pockets, across platforms
- 🔁 Stay curious - new patterns, better tools, cleaner builds
